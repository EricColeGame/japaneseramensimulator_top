## Menu Design Is the Strategic Core

Of the verified features, the player-designed menu is the one with the most mechanical weight. Because you build the menu rather than inherit a fixed one, your choices shape everything downstream — what you prep, what you cook, and how quickly you can serve.

Wild Dog has not published a recipe list with exact quantities or prices, and the store page does not include a code system for unlocking dishes. As a result, recipe guidance should stay at the level of general cooking logic and player practice, not fixed numbers. Practically speaking, the reasoning most players apply is:

1. **Start narrow.** A short menu reduces the number of ingredients your team must prep and track.
2. **Favor overlaps.** Dishes that share base components tend to be easier to run under pressure than dishes that each demand unique prep.
3. **Add breadth later.** Once the team is comfortable with the loop, expanding the menu tests whether the shop can absorb the extra steps.

These are player observations about how menu design typically works in co-op cooking games, not instructions from the developer. The specific dishes available in Japanese Ramen Simulator are not documented on the official page.
